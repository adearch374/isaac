Our History & Journey

From Humble Beginnings to Vision for Global Excellence

LA-PAIX COLLEGE began its journey on 23 September 2019 with just three students and three teachers. At the time, the school was small in size, but the dream behind it was much bigger.

The founding team consisted of Mrs. Temitope Oluwaseun Olaitan, Mr. Onah Benard Egbere and Mr. Emmanuel, under the leadership and proprietorship of Mr. Adewale Paul, the Proprietor, and Mrs. Adewale Remilekun, the Proprietress.

Those early days required courage, sacrifice, patience and a strong belief in what the school could eventually become. The founders were not simply establishing another school; they were beginning a journey to create an educational environment where children could receive quality education, develop good character, discover their abilities and prepare meaningfully for the future.

Today, as LA-PAIX COLLEGE continues to grow, we look back at those humble beginnings with gratitude. The three pioneer students and the founding members of staff will forever remain an important part of the history of the institution.

Our Vision

To build an institution recognised for academic excellence, character and discipline, professionalism, innovation, technology, practical education, leadership development, student welfare, strong parent partnership and global competence.

📋

Our Mission

We provide an environment in which every student can discover who they are, what they can become and how they can contribute to society. We combine knowledge, character, skills, creativity, confidence and responsibility to develop the whole child.

💡

Our Philosophy

Education is more than passing examinations. True education should prepare a child for life—developing students intellectually, morally, socially, emotionally and practically to think independently, communicate effectively, solve problems, work with others and make positive contributions to society.
